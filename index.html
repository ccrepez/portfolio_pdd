Selected Work

A collection of moments captured across events, portraits, performances, and personal projects — organized by story.

Event Documentation March 2026

GDG Build with AI × IWD 2026

One of the most energizing events I've had the chance to document. Google Developer Groups held this special edition in line with International Women's Day — and the energy in the room was something else. I moved between sponsor booths, speaker sessions, and participant activities, trying to catch both the quiet product details and the louder, candid moments of connection and curiosity.

Portrait Photography 2025–2026

Artupic Portrait Series

With Artupic's creative team, I explored editorial portrait work in urban and natural settings around Surabaya. The goal was always authenticity — catching real expressions in natural light, sometimes with a little creative direction, but never forcing a feeling. These shoots remind me why I love portrait work: you get to witness someone be fully themselves for a moment.

Ceremony August 2024

17 August Independence Day

Photographing Indonesia's Independence Day ceremony at SMA Santa Maria was a lesson in patience and timing. The flag-raising, the marching processions, the cultural dress — every frame needed to feel respectful and genuine. I paid close attention to faces: the pride, the concentration, the quiet emotion that comes with marking a national moment together.

Sports Documentation June 2024

Class Meeting Sports

Inter-class basketball and futsal at full intensity. The challenge here was purely technical — freezing the right decisive moment without losing the energy of the game. I loved the unpredictability of it: the mid-air jumps, the celebration, the rivalry all compressed into a single shutter click.

Stage Photography November 2025

Final Project Showcase

Stage photography is its own kind of challenge — theatrical lighting, fast movement, and no second chances. The Final Project Showcase at SMA Santa Maria featured traditional dance, drama, choir, and cultural performances. I worked with what the stage gave me, chasing silhouettes and bursts of colour through each act.

Personal Project Ongoing

Surabaya Skies

This one started as a habit and became an obsession. Surabaya's skies are quietly dramatic — storm clouds rolling in over palm trees, golden hours that last about four minutes, deep blue evenings that feel like they belong somewhere else. I keep shooting them because they never repeat.

Objects & Architecture Ongoing

Things I Notice

Not everything worth photographing is a person or an event. Sometimes it's a texture on a wall, light falling through a window, or the way a building looks at a certain hour. This is my running collection of objects and structures that caught my eye — details most people walk past without a second glance.

Scenery Ongoing

Places I've Been

Landscapes and scenes collected along the way — places that made me stop and reach for my camera. Each one felt worth keeping: a view, a moment of stillness, a scene that captured somewhere or something exactly as it was.

About Me

Telling stories one frame at a time

Hi, I'm Christa — a passionate visual creator with an interest in photography, visual storytelling, and capturing meaningful moments through the lens. From portraits to event documentation, I create images that reflect emotion, atmosphere, and authentic human connections.

Active in campus organizations and creative communities, I've documented Google Developer events, school ceremonies, performance showcases, and editorial portrait projects — always aiming to preserve stories in a visually honest way.
